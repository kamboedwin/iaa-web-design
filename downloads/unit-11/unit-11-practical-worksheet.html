IIA Web Design • Unit 11

Trace an Information Service

Choose a fictional library or records centre. The aim is to understand how a visitor finds information and which system supports the task.

Possible library task: find a book and its shelf location. Possible records task: find the public procedure for requesting a record.

1. Define the need

2. Choose an appropriate system

3. Consider information quality and access

4. Check your response

Can the visitor follow your steps? Does the chosen system match the information needed? Have you avoided disclosing restricted records?

This exercise uses fictional scenarios. No personal or confidential records are required.
