Assignments

Simple assignments build confidence from one concept to the next. Submission dates and links will be published when available.

Unit 1 • availableSubmission to be announced

Assignment 1 — Explore a website and make a basic page

Part A: choose a public library or records-service website. Write the URL, intended users and two useful features (about 100 words).

Part B: create a fictional index.html with a heading, a paragraph and a list of at least three services; take a screenshot in your browser.

Prepare: your short website check, HTML file and screenshot.
